# Quick start

In a few steps an ESP32 becomes a device on the [portal.idryer.org](https://portal.idryer.org/) portal and in the iDryer app: it joins the network, links to your account, shows its data and accepts commands from its card.

You need: an ESP32-C3 board (DevKit, Super Mini or compatible), a USB cable, PlatformIO, a phone with the iDryer app, a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network and a portal account.
